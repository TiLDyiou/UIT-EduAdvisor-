# Privacy Policy (v1.0-2026-05)

Version string for consent records: `v1.0-2026-05`

This document is the mandatory disclosure shown before first-time credential storage, aligned with product requirements (UIT EduAdvisor PRD).

## 1. Account and identity

### Encrypted storage

Your student ID (MSSV) and password are stored only in encrypted form. They exist solely to enable automated synchronization from Moodle and related institutional systems so your experience stays seamless.

### Temporary session data

Session identifiers (cookies) from DAA or Moodle exist only in temporary server memory for the duration of a sync request. They are not written to the database.

## 2. Control over your data

### Right to delete

You may delete your stored password and related personal data at any time from Settings. After confirmation, that data is removed from our systems according to the deletion procedures in place at that time.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

### No commercial resale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

### Technical exception (AI Mate)

To operate AI Mate, some contextual data may be sent to third-party APIs (e.g. Google Gemini). This is disclosed in the feature UI and is subject to the security practices of the API provider.

## 4. Chat content

### Raw chat on server

Detailed chat transcripts between you and AI Mate are not stored on our servers by default.

### Summaries

Only AI-generated summaries may be stored server-side to preserve continuity across sessions. Summaries are semantic abstractions and are not intended to reproduce raw message content verbatim.
