2026-06-24: Section-aware chunking (policy_ingest.py)

Decisions not in spec

  • TOC detection heuristic expanded: The spec said to skip TOC by finding the first CHƯƠNG line without ... dots. In practice, the PDF's TOC has some CHƯƠNG lines (e.g. CHƯƠNG 4) that use spaces instead of dots before the page number. Added a second check: lines ending with a standalone page number (\s+\d{1,3}\s*$) are also treated as TOC lines. This correctly separates all 6 TOC entries from the 6 body entries.

Tradeoffs

  • The page-number heuristic (\s+\d{1,3}\s*$) could theoretically match a body CHƯƠNG title ending in a number <1000. In practice Vietnamese legal document chapter titles are descriptive text, not numbers, so this is safe.
